## Verdict on “Copy it onto a small card and leave it next to the scene.”

**Change it.** Two problems: (1) “next to the scene” is not true for many ideas — the elf *holds* the card or the envelope (25, 26, 29), the card goes *outside* the jar (8) or *on the fridge door* (56), the coupon goes *in the mug* (60), the tag is *tied to the box* (33); (2) “copy it onto” sounds stiff in US English. Proposed:

> **Write it on a small {kind} and leave it with the elf.**

It is true for every paper kind (card, sign, tag, coupon, mission card, question card) and keeps the same rule (only paper kinds, never the mirror or the checklist). For the three ideas with a free printable (25, 29, 60) the “Printable” callout above already points to the sheet, so no extra wording is needed.
